Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

business-expenses ("the app") is a private tool operated by Oren Gaifman to track the business expenses of a two-partner business. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly to search the owner's own mailbox for billing e-mails (receipts and invoices) from service providers. From those e-mails it reads the sender, subject, date, amount, currency, invoice or receipt number, and attached PDF invoices.

How the data is used

  • Only to record business expenses, show monthly totals, and calculate how costs are shared between the partners.
  • The app never sends, deletes, labels or changes e-mails. Its access is read-only.
  • The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person other than the owner reads the data. Data is not transferred to anyone.

Limited Use

business-expenses' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

  • Extracted billing details and invoice PDFs are stored in a database on the owner's own computer. They are not stored on any public server.
  • The Google authorization token is stored encrypted. No passwords are stored.
  • The private dashboard is not reachable from the internet. This website contains no expense data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the owner needs it for bookkeeping. The owner can disconnect Gmail in the app at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local database removes all stored data.

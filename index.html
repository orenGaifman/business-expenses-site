₪business-expenses

A private expense tracker for a two-partner business

business-expenses is a small, private tool run by Oren Gaifman. It keeps track of the monthly costs of the software services our business uses (cloud hosting, AI APIs and similar subscriptions) and how those costs are shared between the two business partners.

What it does

  • Finds billing receipts and invoices from service providers in the owner's own Gmail inbox, using read-only access.
  • Reads only the billing details needed for bookkeeping: provider, date, amount, currency and invoice number.
  • Shows the monthly total and each partner's share in a private dashboard that runs on the owner's computer.

Who it is for

Only the owner. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and no one else's Google account is connected. The dashboard itself is not on the internet.

Your data

Gmail access is read-only. The tool never sends, deletes or changes e-mails, never shares data with anyone, and never uses it for advertising or to train AI models. Full details are in the privacy policy.
